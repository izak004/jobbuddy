Where JobBuddy looks for jobs

  • Remotive — public remote-job board, no account needed
  • Jobicy — public remote-job board, no account needed
  • Himalayas — public remote-job board, no account needed
  • We Work Remotely — public remote-job board, no account needed
  • Working Nomads — public remote-job board, no account needed
  • SimplyHired — scanned directly since it has no public listing feed; usually the richest source
  • Adzuna (optional) — general job aggregator, enable it below
  • Company career pages (optional) — specific companies you add yourself in sources/companies.json, checked directly so their newest postings aren't missed. Supports Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Recruitee, Workable, and Workday — including several major pharma companies (Pfizer, Sanofi, AstraZeneca, MSD, ProPharma Group) pre-configured by default

Sites that actively block automated access — Monster, CareerBuilder — aren't included, and FlexJobs is skipped since it requires a paid account to see full listings.

Enable Adzuna (optional)

Free API key, instant signup at developer.adzuna.com — look for "App ID" and "App Key" on your account page. Leave both blank to skip this source entirely.

Anthropic API key

Powers the "tailor resume to this job" feature. Job discovery itself is free and doesn't need this.

💲 Approximate cost: $0.03–$0.08 per tailored resume (you're charged once each time you click "Update resume according to this job role" on a job — searching, browsing, and tracking jobs costs nothing). $5 of credit covers roughly 60-150 tailored resumes.

Don't have a key yet? Here's how to get one:

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign up (or log in if you already have an account).
  2. In the left sidebar, click API Keys (under Settings).
  3. Click Create Key, give it any name (e.g. "JobBuddy"), and confirm.
  4. Copy the key it shows you — it starts with sk-ant- and is only shown once, so copy it before closing that screen.
  5. In the same sidebar, click Billing and add a small amount of credit (even $5 goes a long way — tailoring one resume costs only a few cents).
  6. Paste the key into the field above and click "Save changes" at the bottom of this page.

Job titles

A listing only ever shows up if its title matches one of your interested titles, and only if it doesn't match an excluded one.

Type a title or keyword and press Enter. These also become the search terms sent to job boards.

Titles containing any of these are hidden, even if they'd otherwise match.

Location & work authorization

This app defaults to the US job market — all of its built-in job sources and matching logic are tuned around US-based remote listings. You can change the target country below, but discovery quality outside the US hasn't been tested.

Remote only

When on, onsite listings and remote-in-a-different-country listings are both hidden.

Employment type & freshness

Listings with an undetectable employment type are always shown — this only excludes ones explicitly labeled otherwise.
